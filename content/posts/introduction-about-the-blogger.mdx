Sharaaf Nazeer is an experienced Computer Software professional with 9+ years of expertise in various areas such as software product engineering, enterprise application development, cloud development and architecture, technology research and design, and business involvement. He has extensive experience in managing software product development teams, ranging from nascent start-ups to the largest companies worldwide, including the Singapore, USA, South Africa, Netherlands, New Zealand, Malaysia, Indonesia, and Sri Lanka.  
Having a strong grasp of algorithms, data structures, and programming languages as a software development professional, he possesses extensive knowledge of programming languages and frameworks such as Java, NodeJS, JavaScript, React, Golang, and more. Along with his technical skills, he actively participates in managerial decision-making processes, training and development activities, and recruitment programs.  
With a Master's degree in Computer Science from the University of Colombo, he is a driven and enthusiastic ideologist, who relies on teamwork and leadership as his key pillars of success.  
Outside of work, he is a fun-filled person who likes to travel, hang out with friends, and watch movies.
