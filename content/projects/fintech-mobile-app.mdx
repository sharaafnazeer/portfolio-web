A consumer remittance product for sending money across 14 corridors in
Southeast Asia. The product brief was simple: "make it the fastest, most
forgiving app on the market". The implementation was less simple.

## Constraints we designed for

- **Low-end Android devices** (1 GB RAM, Android 8). Animations and image
  assets were sized down brutally; the entire app fits in 18MB.
- **Flaky networks**. Every call retries with exponential backoff and idempotency
  keys so a customer never sends the same transfer twice.
- **Compliance + KYC** in 4 jurisdictions with different document
  requirements — a single dynamic-form engine, not a switch statement.
- **Offline-first UX**: the user can compose a transfer offline, and the app
  sends it when connectivity returns with clear status updates.

## Outcome

By v1.4 the app had a 4.7-star rating across both stores and a p95 "send a
transfer" time of 28 seconds, end-to-end, including KYC checks. It was
shipped across two countries with two more in pilot when I rolled off.
